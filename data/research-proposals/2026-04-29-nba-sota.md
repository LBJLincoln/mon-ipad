## Key Papers

### 1. Uncertainty-Aware ML for NBA Forecasting (MDPI Information 2026)
- **URL:** https://www.mdpi.com/2078-2489/17/1/56
- **Architecture:** RNN + Monte Carlo dropout for calibrated sequential probabilities
- **Features:** Team-level metrics + rolling-form indicators + spatial shot-chart embeddings
- **Validation:** Strict chronological partitioning (train ≤2022, val 2023, test 2024); cross-season robustness 2012–2024
- **Results:**
  - Logistic regression baseline: **Brier 0.199** ← SOTA tabular
  - XGBoost: **Brier 0.202**
  - RNN-MC (best): calibrated sequential probabilities

### 2. Stacked Ensemble for NBA (Nature Scientific Reports 2025)
- **URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-13657-1
- **Architecture:** Stacking (NB, AdaBoost, MLP, KNN, XGBoost, DT, LR as base learners)
- **Results:** CNN Brier 0.221; SVM accuracy 0.7749 (best)

### 3. Comparative ML for NBA (IEEE Xplore 2026)
- **URL:** https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11030489/
- **Results:** AutoGluon 0.7738, DNN 0.7726, LR 0.7743 accuracy
