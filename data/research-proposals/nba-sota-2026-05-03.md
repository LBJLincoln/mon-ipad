3. **Uncertainty quantification** (MC Dropout / conformal prediction) — not in our CPU island pipeline. Jan 2026 paper shows calibration improvement from Brier 0.225 → 0.218 with uncertainty-aware training.
4. **Post-hoc isotonic calibration** — already used in TabICL (0.22054). Not yet applied to island GA evaluation loop.
