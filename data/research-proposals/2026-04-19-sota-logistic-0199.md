## Why Logistic Regression Beats Tree Ensembles on NBA

1. NBA home/away outcomes have near-linear decision boundaries in feature space
2. L1 regularization naturally enforces sparsity (50-100 features out of 3365)
3. Calibration is inherently better — sigmoid output is probability-native
4. Tree ensembles tend to under-calibrate at tails (Brier penalizes tails heavily)
