## Key Finding: Uncertainty-Aware RNN Achieves Brier ~0.20

**Source:** "Uncertainty-Aware Machine Learning for NBA Forecasting in Digital Betting Markets" — MDPI Information 17(1):56, January 2026
**URL:** https://www.mdpi.com/2078-2489/17/1/56

### Architecture
- Recurrent neural network (RNN) with Monte Carlo dropout for uncertainty quantification
- Input features: team-level performance metrics, rolling-form indicators, spatial shot-chart embeddings
- Evaluation: Brier score ~0.20, log-loss ~0.58, AUC 0.75-0.76
- Benchmark: XGBoost + logistic regression on same data also achieved Brier ~0.20

### Relevance to Nomos42 Fleet
- **Target gap closed**: Our fleet best is 0.22085 (S17). Paper shows 0.20 is achievable with XGBoost on 2024 data.
- **Feature set overlap**: Rolling-form indicators and shot-chart embeddings are already in our engine.py cat51+ features
- **Key insight**: XGBoost alone achieved ~0.20 — the RNN is NOT required. Tree models CAN reach target.
