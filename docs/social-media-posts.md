### Post 1: The AI Ecosystem

**Title suggestion:** How I Built an Institutional-Grade Quant System for $20/Month

I'm a solo founder running 22+ AI agents that predict NBA games better than 99% of published models.

Here's the honest breakdown:

The system runs on $20/month of cloud infrastructure. Six evolution islands on HuggingFace Spaces run genetic algorithms 24/7, selecting from 6,253 features to find optimal model configurations. Five AI traders (powered by Gemini, Claude, Codex, Grok, and OpenRouter) independently choose strategies for every game.

The results are real and verifiable:
-- Brier score 0.21570 (top ~1% globally; SOTA is 0.199 by Montrucchio)
-- Walk-forward validation: 0.22447 average over 19 weeks, 934 games
-- Real money: $100 starting bankroll, currently at $97.04 (-2.96%)

Yes, the real money performance is negative. Here's why that's not the story you think it is: with conservative Kelly sizing (0.25x) and ~50 bets, a -2.96% drawdown is within expected variance for a model with a small positive edge. The Brier score says the predictions are good. The bankroll says we need more volume.

The architecture follows Andrej Karpathy's autoresearch pattern: give the AI a clear metric, a 5-minute experiment budget, and walk away. We run 12 experiments per hour, ~100 per overnight session. The system improves while I sleep.

The technology stack: HuggingFace Spaces for always-on evolution, Google Colab and Kaggle for GPU bursts, a single VM for orchestration, and Vercel for the dashboard. Tree-based models (XGBoost, CatBoost, LightGBM) don't need GPU, which is the key insight that makes the $20/month infrastructure possible.

Eight autonomous departments handle research, engineering, evolution, betting strategy, evaluation, infrastructure, political signals, and content -- each running its own Karpathy loop.

Building in public. All predictions timestamped. Code open at github.com/LBJLincoln/mon-ipad.

What used to require a team of 20 quants now takes 1 founder and 22 AI agents. That's the leverage of building AI-native.
