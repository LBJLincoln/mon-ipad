We built an autonomous AI system that outperforms NBA betting markets.

After 994 backtested games across the full 2025-26 season, our prediction engine achieves a Brier score of 0.215 — significantly better than the market average of ~0.25.

Here's what makes it different:

**The Architecture:**
- 46 feature categories with 6,253 raw features per game
- 11 competing prediction models (XGBoost, CatBoost, LightGBM, Extra-Trees, and more)
- 6 evolution islands running genetic algorithms 24/7 on HuggingFace
- Autonomous research loop: 12 experiments per hour, following Andrej Karpathy's autoresearch pattern

**The Competition Engine:**
5 AI agents (powered by Gemini, Claude, Codex, Grok, and OpenRouter) compete in a simulated trading floor. Each agent independently selects models, strategies, and bet sizing for every game — then we let Darwin decide.
