# La Forge — Psychological Onboarding & Retention Framework

> Every message is calibrated to maximize engagement, trust, and conversion.
> Based on: Fogg Behavior Model, Hook Model, Loss Aversion, Endowment Effect

## Phase 1: FIRST CONTACT (0-5 min)

### Hook Message (when user sends /start to @Forge42Bot)

```
Welcome to La Forge 🔥

You have an idea. We have 7 AI agents ready to turn it into a real product.

Tell me your idea in one sentence — I'll show you exactly how we'd build it.

No code needed. No technical skills. Just your vision.
```

**Psychology:**
- Immediate value proposition (7 agents = authority)
- Zero barrier ("one sentence", "no code")
- Curiosity gap ("I'll show you exactly how")
- Identity reinforcement ("your vision")

### After User Shares Idea (Agent 0 responds)

```
Interesting. Here's what I see:

📊 Market: [auto-generated niche insight]
🎯 Target: [auto-generated user persona summary]
💰 Revenue potential: [range estimate]
⚡ Time to MVP: [estimate]

Your Product Brief is ready. Here's what happens next:

→ Agent 1 (Product Builder) starts your MVP
→ Agent 2 (Business Strategist) scans your market
→ Agent 3 (Communication) plans your launch

Want to activate? You have 5 free messages today to explore.
```

**Psychology:**
- Instant gratification (immediate analysis)
- Specificity creates trust (numbers, ranges)
- Endowment effect ("Your Product Brief is ready" — they already own it)
- Scarcity ("5 free messages today")
- Progress illusion (next steps already defined)

```

**Psychology:**

- **Day 3:** FOMO + scarcity (others are launching, window closing)
- **Day 5:** Loss aversion (here's what you'd HAVE by now)
- **Day 7:** Deadline + endowment ("your brief expires" — they own it)

### Win Messages (trigger on milestones)

```

Revenue: $[X]

```

That puts you in the top [Y]% of Forge products.

```

### Risk Messages (trigger on disengagement)

**7 days no interaction:**
```
Hey, your agents are idle.

They've been waiting for your input on [specific decision].
Meanwhile, your competitors shipped [X] updates.

Quick decision needed: [yes/no question about the product]
→ Just reply YES or NO
```

**14 days no interaction:**
```
Your agents have been running maintenance mode for 2 weeks.

If you need a break, that's OK. Your agents will keep monitoring.
But your growth has stalled:
- [metric] dropped [X]%
- [competitors] gained [Y] users

One message reactivates everything: /reactivate
```

**30 days (churn risk):**
```
Your Forge subscription renews in 3 days.

Your agents built: [summary of everything created]
Total value delivered: $[estimated]

If you cancel, all agents go offline and your space is archived after 30 days.

→ /keep — agents stay active
→ /pause — $0/mo, space archived, resume anytime
→ /cancel — everything stops
```

**Psychology:**
- **Win messages:** Dopamine hits, social comparison, projection
- **Risk messages:** Loss aversion ("competitors shipped"), minimal effort ("YES or NO")
- **Churn messages:** Sunk cost ("everything created"), graceful exit options (reduces resentment)

## Psychological Triggers Matrix

| Trigger | Where Used | Mechanism |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| **Loss aversion** | Day 3, 5, 7, churn | "Window closing", "competitors ahead", "brief expires" |
| **Endowment effect** | Day 1, upgrade | "YOUR brief", "YOUR agents", "YOUR product" |
| **Scarcity** | Free tier | "5 messages today", "expires in 24h" |
| **Social proof** | Day 3, milestones | "12 others launched", "top Y% of Forge products" |
| **Anchoring** | Pricing | "$200/mo but saves $X at your revenue" |
| **Reciprocity** | Free tier | Free analysis → obligation to upgrade |
| **Progress illusion** | Onboarding | Next steps pre-defined, agents "already working" |
| **Commitment escalation** | Free→Builder→Factory | Small yes → bigger yes → full commitment |
| **IKEA effect** | Product building | Users who participate in creation value it more |
| **Zeigarnik effect** | Retention | Incomplete milestones create tension to continue |

## Anti-Patterns (NEVER do)

1. **Never guilt-trip** — always frame as opportunity, not obligation
2. **Never spam** — max 1 push notification per day
3. **Never lie about metrics** — all numbers must be real or clearly estimated
4. **Never make cancellation hard** — /cancel works instantly (builds trust)
5. **Never compare users** — each journey is unique, only compare to their own past
6. **Never pressure on Day 0** — let the free experience speak for itself
